Refetch is the front page for builders: an open-source alternative to YC-controlled Hacker News, with curated tech news and the discussions around it.

Stories come from trusted sources across AI, startups, open source, security, science and programming, and are ranked by a transparent algorithm you can read on GitHub. The community votes, comments and shares the rest.

FEATURES
• Top, New and Show: today's front page, the latest stories, and what people are building
• Discussions: threaded comments and replies on every story
• Voting: upvote and downvote stories and comments
• Share a story: post a link with a note that starts the conversation
• Share anywhere: send a story's discussion to friends from any app
• Notifications: replies to you, and an optional weekly digest of top stories
• Light and dark themes, in the Refetch purple

Refetch is free, open source and built on Appwrite. Anonymous usage analytics help us improve the app, and you can turn them off any time in Account.

Learn more at https://refetch.io
